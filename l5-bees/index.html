Card 1 · Find the empty cells

How many cells in the data are empty?

  1. Click cell O1. Type Empty cells and press Enter.
  2. Click cell P1. Type this exactly, then press Enter:
    =COUNTBLANK(A2:M4166)
  3. Type the number from P1 in the box below.
Hint 1

COUNTBLANK counts empty cells. A2:M4166 means "every bee, every column." Row 1 is the column names, so we start at row 2.

Hint 2

If you see the formula as text, check that it starts with = and has no space in front.

Solution

3 empty cells.

Now find them and fill them in.

  1. Click cell A1. Click the Data tab. Click Filter.Small arrows in every column name.
  2. Click the arrow in K1 (Common name).
  3. Uncheck (Select All). Scroll to the bottom. Check (Blanks). Click OK.Only one bee is left: Specimen 1502182.
  4. Look at that bee's row. Cells K, L, and M are empty. Type in them:
    K: Sweat bee · L: Brittany Owens · M: nd
  5. Click the arrow in K1 again. Click Clear Filter From "Common name".All the bees are back. Cell P1 now says 0.
Hint · Why "Sweat bee"?

Look at column J (Scientific name). This bee is a Lasioglossum. In this file, 988 other Lasioglossum bees are called Sweat bee. The bee next to it (1502183) is the same kind and was identified by Brittany Owens. We use what the data already tells us.

Solution

All 3 empty cells were on one bee, Specimen 1502182 (row 2086). After the fix, P1 = 0.

Card 2 · Count the bees by sex

How many female, male, and queen bees are there?

  1. Type these labels in column O:
    O3 Female · O4 Male · O5 Queen · O6 No data · O7 Total
  2. Type these formulas in column P:
    P3 =COUNTIF(H:H,"F")
    P4 =COUNTIF(H:H,"M")
    P5 =COUNTIF(H:H,"Q")
    P6 =COUNTIF(H:H,"nd")
    P7 =SUM(P3:P6)
  3. Type your numbers in the boxes below.
Hint 1

COUNTIF counts cells that match. H:H means "all of column H," the Sex column. "F" is what we look for. Text in a formula always needs quotes.

Hint 2

Seeing #NAME? means a quote mark is missing. Each letter needs a quote on both sides: "F".

Solution

Female 2,624 · Male 358 · Queen 41 · No data 1,138 · Total 4,161

Detective question: the file has 4,165 bees. Your total is 4,161. Where are the missing 4?

  1. Click the arrow in H1 (Sex). Look at the list of values.
  2. Find the strange one. Then click Cancel.
Hint 1

Two of the values in the filter list look almost the same. Look very closely at the space in front of them.

Hint 2

Remember the SAT/ACT file? " Maryland" had a hidden space in front. That is the same problem here.

Solution

4 cells say  F (a space, then F). COUNTIF looks for an exact match, so "F" does not count them. The real female count is 2,624 + 4 = 2,628. You can fix this in Extra B.

Card 3 · Count the bumble bees

How many bumble bees did the team catch?

  1. O9: type bumble bee (exact). P9: type
    =COUNTIF(K:K,"bumble bee")
  2. Look at P9. Is that number right? Type it in the first box.
  3. O10: type bumble (any). P10: type
    =COUNTIF(K:K,"*bumble*")
  4. Type the new number in the second box.
Hint 1 · Why is the first one 0?

Filter column K and type bumble in the search box. The names are longer, like "American bumble bee." No cell says only "bumble bee."

Hint 2 · What does the star do?

The star * means "any text here." "*bumble*" means "bumble with anything before it or after it."

Solution

Exact match: 0. With the stars: 474.

That is American bumble bee 314 + Common eastern bumble bee 80 + Brown belted Bumble Bee 79 + two spotted bumblebee 1.

Extra B · Fix the 4 hidden spaces

Fix the 4 " F" cells so the total matches 4,165.

  1. Click the letter H at the very top of column H. The whole column turns gray.
  2. Press Ctrl + H. The Find and Replace box opens.
  3. Find what: press the Space bar once, then type F.
  4. Replace with: type F (no space).
  5. Click Options. Check Match entire cell contents. Click Replace All."We made 4 replacements." Click OK, then Close.
Hint · Why "Match entire cell contents"?

Without it, Excel changes a space-F inside longer text too. With it, Excel only changes cells that are exactly " F".

Solution

Female = 2,628. Total = 4,165. Every bee is counted now. Save with Ctrl + S.
