1 What the final project is

Your final project is a data story in Excel.

You work in pairs. Two students per group.

You use Excel only.

Your workbook has these sheets:

  • Raw Data. The data as it came. You do not change it.
  • Cleaning. Use Format as Table. Fix what is wrong or missing in the data. Use at least one explore or clean function, such as COUNTIF, IF or TRIM. Use at least one math function, such as SUM or AVERAGE.
  • Final. The cleaned data, with Paste Values.

Then you build PivotTables and PivotCharts that answer one plain-English question.

You put the charts together on one dashboard sheet.

You present it live in class on Tuesday, Dec 8. Your presentation is 5 to 8 minutes.

You do not need to know the answers yet. You are choosing a topic you can talk about for 5 to 8 minutes.

Miami 311 requests, March 2024

File name: final-project-miami-311-march-2024.csv

What it is

Requests that people in the City of Miami sent to the city's 311 line in March 2024. Each row shows the department that handled the request and how many days it took.

Size

4,205 rows and 14 columns. One row is one request.

Columns

ColumnWhat it means
ticket_idThe ID of the request, such as 24-10082500.
issue_typeA short code for the kind of request.
issue_DescriptionThe kind of request, in words.
case_ownerThe city department that handles it.
zip_codeThe ZIP code where the request is from. It is a label, not an amount. One row has ZIP code 0.
DistrictThe city district number. It is a label, not an amount.
ticket_created_date_timeThe date and time the request came in. Excel shows it as a date and time.
ticket_closed_date_timeThe date and time the request was closed. A few rows are empty.
ticket_statusWhere the request stands now: closed, open, cancelled and so on.
method_receivedHow the request came in. PHONE is a phone call. WEB is the website. IPHONE and ANDROID are phone apps. EMAIL is email. The other values are codes.
goal_daysThe number of days the city aims to finish this kind of request.
actual_completed_daysThe number of days the request took.
Ticket_PriorityHow urgent the request was marked. These are short codes.
OverDueFlagThe city's own overdue mark. 1 means overdue. 0 means not overdue. Use it as given. A few rows with 0 still show more days than the goal.

3 questions you could answer

  1. Which three kinds of requests came in most often? Which department handles them?
  2. Which district waits the longest, on average, for a request to be closed?
  3. Do requests sent by web or app get closed faster or slower than phone calls?

Heads up

When you average actual_completed_days, use only requests with ticket_status CLOSED. Other statuses carry numbers that will give you the wrong average.

Use the column as it is. Do not work out the days yourself.

District 0 has very few requests. Look at how many requests are behind an average before you trust it.

Some request types are about animals. You may filter them out if you want. In issue_Description, search for the word ANIMAL. If you filter, say so on your Cleaning sheet.

Starbucks drinks nutrition

File name: final-project-starbucks-drinks.csv

What it is

Nutrition facts for Starbucks drinks, from the official Starbucks nutrition guide. Each row is one drink in one size, with one milk choice and whip or no whip.

Size

1,147 rows and 15 columns. One row is one drink, in one size, with one milk choice.

Columns

ColumnWhat it means
product_nameThe name of the drink. Some names are all lowercase and some are capitalized. Some have accents, such as Caffè.
sizeThe size of the drink. Most are short, tall, grande, venti or trenta. A few say solo, doppio, triple, quad, 1 scoop or 1 shot.
milkThe kind of milk, as a code. 0 is none. 1 is nonfat. 2 is 2%. 3 is soy. 4 is coconut. 5 is whole.
whipWhipped cream, as a code. 1 means whip was added. 0 means no whip.
serv_size_m_lThe serving size in milliliters.
caloriesThe calories in the drink. Some drinks show 0.
total_fat_gTotal fat, in grams.
saturated_fat_gSaturated fat, in grams.
trans_fat_gTrans fat, in grams.
cholesterol_mgCholesterol, in milligrams.
sodium_mgSodium, in milligrams.
total_carbs_gTotal carbohydrates, in grams.
fiber_gFiber, in grams.
sugar_gSugar, in grams.
caffeine_mgCaffeine, in milligrams.

3 questions you could answer

  1. Which kinds of drinks, such as Frappuccino, latte or tea, have the most sugar in a grande?
  2. How much do the calories change when the same drink is made with whole milk, nonfat milk or soy milk?
  3. Which sizes and drinks give the most caffeine for the calories?

Heads up

The milk and whip columns are codes. Turn them into words so your PivotTable shows names, not numbers.

The file has no drink type column. You build one from product_name, for example Latte or Frappuccino.

The cleaning here is mostly turning codes into words. You still show your cleaning on the Cleaning sheet. Plan to add helper columns, for example a Milk Type column.

The same drink shows up in several sizes and with several milks. Look at how many rows are behind an average before you trust it.

Some drinks show 0 calories. Decide whether to keep them, and say what you decided on your Cleaning sheet.

College tuition and costs

File name: final-project-college-tuition.csv

What it is

What US colleges and universities charge for one school year, for students who live in the state and students who come from out of state.

Size

2,973 rows and 10 columns. One row is one school.

Columns

ColumnWhat it means
nameThe name of the school.
stateThe state the school is in. It says NA for Puerto Rico, DC and a few territories. The state_code column is filled in for those.
state_codeThe two-letter code for the state.
typePublic, Private or For Profit. One school says Other.
degree_length2 Year or 4 Year. One school says Other.
room_and_boardThe yearly cost of housing and meals.
in_state_tuitionThe yearly tuition for students from the same state.
in_state_totalTuition plus room and board for students from the same state. If room_and_board is NA, this is just the tuition.
out_of_state_tuitionThe yearly tuition for students from another state.
out_of_state_totalTuition plus room and board for students from another state. If room_and_board is NA, this is just the tuition.

3 questions you could answer

  1. Which states have the highest average in-state tuition at 4-year public schools?
  2. How much more is out-of-state tuition than in-state tuition at public schools, compared with private schools?
  3. How does in-state tuition at 2-year schools compare with 4-year schools, and how many schools are in each group?

Heads up

Some schools have the text NA in room_and_board, because they do not list housing. If you drag that column into Values, the PivotTable counts it instead of adding it up. Decide how to handle the NA before you use it.

This file is fairly tidy. You still show your cleaning on the Cleaning sheet. Start with the NA values.

Many 2-year schools have no room and board. When you compare 2-year and 4-year schools, use tuition, not totals.
