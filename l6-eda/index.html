Video Game Sales Explorer

The story: We have sales data for 16,715 video games, from 1980 to 2016. One row is one game on one console.

Global_Sales is the money the game made around the world, in millions of dollars. For example, 82.53 means 82.53 million.

This lesson takes 3 days. Today is Day 1. Day 2 and Day 3 will appear on this page later.

Day 1
CORE

Part 1 · Set up the data

Keep only the columns we need, make a table, and remove 2 games with no name.

  1. Go to Canvas. Download lec6-video_games_sales.csv. Open it from your Downloads folder.
  2. File → Save As → Browse → Desktop. Name: Games-YourLastName. Save as type: Excel Workbook (*.xlsx). Click Save.The top of Excel says Games-YourLastName.xlsx
  3. Hold Ctrl and click these column letters at the very top: F G H I L M N. Right-click one of them → Delete.9 columns left: Name, Platform, Year_of_Release, Genre, Publisher, Global_Sales, Critic_Score, Developer, Rating.
  4. Click A1 → Home → Format as Table → pick a style → check My table has headers → OK.
  5. Click K1. Type Blank names. Click L1. Type =COUNTBLANK(A2:A16716) and press Enter.
  6. Click the arrow in A1 (Name) → uncheck (Select All) → scroll down → check (Blanks) → OK.2 rows. Both are GEN games from 1993.Select both row numbers on the left. Right-click → Delete Row. Then arrow in A1 → Clear Filter From "Name".L1 now says 0.
  7. Click inside the table. Table Design tab → check Total Row. Click C2, press Ctrl + ↓ to jump to the bottom. In the Total row: Year_of_Release → arrow → Max. Global_Sales → arrow → Average.
Hint 1 · Deleting columns

Click the gray letters at the very top, not the cells. We delete 4 sales columns (NA, EU, JP, Other) and 3 critic/user columns (Critic_Count, User_Score, User_Count). We keep Global_Sales and Critic_Score.

Hint 2 · The Total row

The Total row is at the very bottom, row 16715. If you don't see the arrow in a Total cell, click that cell first. The average shows many decimals, like 0.5335…. Type 2 decimals.

Solution

Blank names: 2. After deleting them, we have 16,713 games.

Max year: 2016. Average Global_Sales: 0.53 million dollars per game.

Build your first PivotTable and learn the 4 boxes: Filters, Columns, Rows, Values.

  1. Click any cell inside your table. Insert → Recommended PivotTables → click the first one → OK.A new sheet. A PivotTable on the left. The Field List on the right.
  2. At the bottom of the Field List, drag fields into the 4 boxes so they match this table. Drag out anything extra.
    BoxField

    ValuesSum of Global_Sales
  3. Click the arrow next to Publisher (above the PivotTable) → click Nintendo → OK.10 consoles down the side, from 3DS to WiiU.
  4. Double-click the sheet tab. Type Pivot Practice. Press Enter. Save.
Hint 1 · The Field List is gone

Click any cell inside the PivotTable. Still gone? PivotTable Analyze tab → Field List.

Hint 2 · Finding the top console

Look at the Grand Total column on the far right. Find the biggest number. The console name is on the left of that row.

Solution

Nintendo Grand Total: 1,788.81 million dollars. Top console: Wii, with 386.25.

Part 3 · Sales by Genre

Guess first: which type of game made the most money in history? Write your guess down.

  1. Go back to the lec6-video_games_sales sheet (the first tab). Click inside the table. Insert → PivotTable → OK.A new, empty PivotTable on a new sheet.
  2. Drag Genre to Rows. Drag Global_Sales to Values.12 genres, from Action to Strategy.
  3. Right-click any number → Sort → Sort Largest to Smallest.
  4. Select the numbers. Home → Comma Style (the , button).Numbers look like 1,745.26
  5. Double-click the header Sum of Global_Sales. In Custom Name, type Global Sales in Millions ($) → OK.
  6. Rename the sheet tab Sales by Genre. Save.
Hint 1 · Sorting

Right-click a number, not a genre name. If you right-click a name, Sort sorts by the alphabet.

Hint 2 · Renaming the header

Double-click exactly on the words Sum of Global_Sales. A box opens. The name goes in the top line, called Custom Name.

Solution

Action wins with 1,745.26 million. Sports 1,332.00. Shooter is #3 with 1,052.94. Lowest is Strategy with 174.50. Grand Total 8,917.53.

Many people guess Shooter. The data says Action. That is EDA checking our guess.
